# Business OS Revenue Growth Loop

Status: PORTABLE SUPER AGENT BLUEPRINT
Branch: `super-agent-blueprint`
Runtime integration: DEFERRED

## Governing idea

Business OS marketing must not optimize for content volume, impressions, followers, clicks, or isolated ROAS. It must operate as a closed-loop business growth system that connects market intelligence to contribution profit and feeds verified results back into the next decision.

The canonical loop is:

```text
BUSINESS BRAIN
    ↓
MARKET INTELLIGENCE
    ↓
STRATEGY
    ↓
OFFER
    ↓
CAMPAIGN HYPOTHESIS
    ↓
CREATIVE INTELLIGENCE
    ↓
CREATIVE / UGC FACTORY
    ↓
WEBSITE / FUNNEL / CONVERSION
    ↓
DISTRIBUTION
    ↓
TRAFFIC
    ↓
LEAD CAPTURE
    ↓
CRM
    ↓
QUALIFICATION / NURTURE
    ↓
SALES
    ↓
CUSTOMER
    ↓
RETENTION / UPSELL / REFERRAL
    ↓
REVENUE
    ↓
GROSS MARGIN / CONTRIBUTION PROFIT
    ↓
ATTRIBUTION
    ↓
EXPERIMENT JUDGE
    ↓
LEARNING
    ↓
NEXT BEST ACTION
    ↺
```

The loop never ends at publishing. Publishing is only an intermediate action.

## North-star economics

The system should progressively optimize:

```text
Revenue
- media spend
- creative production cost
- sales acquisition cost
- variable fulfillment cost
= contribution profit
```

Primary growth metrics:
- contribution profit;
- revenue;
- gross margin;
- CAC;
- LTV;
- LTV:CAC;
- payback period;
- qualified-lead cost;
- opportunity conversion;
- close rate;
- average order/deal value;
- retention and renewal;
- expansion revenue.

Secondary diagnostic metrics:
- CTR;
- CPC;
- CPM;
- landing-page conversion;
- hook retention;
- video watch time;
- form completion;
- booked-call rate;
- email/SMS engagement;
- organic visibility;

Vanity metrics may be recorded but cannot be used alone to declare success.

## Growth organization

### TBrain
Owns shared business context, capability discovery, cross-department mission state, verified learning and routing.

### CEO
Owns company goals, constraints, capital priorities and final cross-functional tradeoffs.

### CMO
Owns demand generation, brand growth, campaign portfolio and marketing efficiency.

### CRO
Owns pipeline, lead handling, sales conversion, forecasting and revenue execution.

### CFO
Owns economics, margin, cash, budget guardrails and profit verification.

### COO
Owns fulfillment readiness, capacity and operational constraints so marketing does not sell what the business cannot deliver.

### Customer Success
Owns retention, renewal, expansion, referral and voice-of-customer signals.

## Marketing machine

The CMO supervises a portfolio of specialist systems. These are capabilities, not necessarily permanent processes.

### 1. Market Intelligence
Responsibilities:
- customer research;
- competitor research;
- category analysis;
- demand and trend analysis;
- review mining;
- community research;
- sales-call objection mining;
- CRM loss-reason mining;
- offer/price observation;
- channel opportunity analysis.

Outputs:
- audience segments;
- jobs-to-be-done;
- pains/desires;
- objections;
- competitor map;
- market opportunities;
- evidence-backed hypotheses.

### 2. Offer Intelligence
Responsibilities:
- define value proposition;
- package offer;
- pricing/discount logic recommendations;
- bonuses;
- guarantees where legally/business appropriate;
- urgency/scarcity rules without deception;
- CTA design;
- offer-to-segment matching.

The offer is versioned so performance can be attributed to the exact offer presented.

### 3. Creative Intelligence
Before producing assets, inspect historical and market evidence.

Creative DNA fields:
- audience;
- pain/desire;
- angle;
- hook;
- promise;
- offer;
- proof;
- format;
- creator/avatar;
- visual style;
- length;
- CTA;
- platform;
- landing page;
- spend;
- impressions;
- clicks;
- qualified leads;
- opportunities;
- revenue;
- contribution profit.

The system maintains a Winning Creative Library and a Losing Pattern Library. Losing evidence is valuable and must not be silently discarded.

### 4. Creative Director
Responsibilities:
- translate strategy into creative briefs;
- choose concepts worth producing;
- maintain brand consistency;
- prevent duplicate concepts;
- coordinate copy, UGC, static, video and web assets;
- judge production quality before publishing.

### 5. UGC Studio
Pipeline:

```text
brief
→ hook variants
→ script variants
→ storyboard / shot list
→ creator/avatar/provider selection
→ product/source footage
→ B-roll
→ voice
→ captions
→ music/audio
→ edit
→ CTA variants
→ platform variants
→ creative QA
→ approval
```

Provider-neutral by design. A Creative Router chooses among approved AI providers, internal tools, stock/source assets or human creators according to quality, cost, speed, rights, brand and campaign needs.

### 6. Content Factory
Produces coordinated long- and short-form content from the same strategy:
- articles;
- landing-page copy;
- social posts;
- short video;
- email;
- SMS;
- newsletters;
- sales enablement;
- lead magnets;
- case studies;
- comparison content;
- FAQs;
- knowledge assets.

One canonical claim/evidence layer should prevent different channels from inventing conflicting facts.

Capabilities:

- competitor analysis;
- UX;
- conversion copy;
- brand/design;

- WordPress/Elementor;

- analytics/tracking;

Visual QA loop:

### 8. Search Intelligence
Unifies traditional and AI discovery:

- schema/structured data;
- AEO;

### 9. Paid Media
Provider-neutral campaign management for approved channels.

Responsibilities:

- campaign structure;
- budget proposals;

- tracking validation;

Responsibilities:

```text

→ visitor/person P-991

→ opportunity O-113

```

This is how Business OS learns which creative, campaign, offer and page actually produced money.

## Experiment Engine

Every meaningful campaign should carry a hypothesis.

Example:

`For homeowners with recent storm exposure, a storm-inspection angle will produce a lower qualified-lead CAC than a generic free-estimate angle.`

Experiment record:
- hypothesis;
- audience;
- variable under test;
- control;
- variants;
- start/end criteria;
- primary metric;
- guardrail metrics;
- minimum evidence rule;
- budget;
- result;
- judge verdict;
- lesson;
- next action.

The system should distinguish correlation from strong causal claims and should not claim a winner without sufficient evidence.

## Attribution Engine

- offer ID;
- landing-page/variant ID;
- lead source;
- opportunity;

Later maturity:

The platform's own ad-reported conversion value is evidence, not unquestioned truth.

## Revenue Optimization Agent

Question it must answer:

`Given current evidence and business constraints, what is the highest-value next allocation of money, creative effort, sales effort and website effort?`

Inputs:
- spend;
- revenue;
- contribution profit;
- CAC;
- LTV;
- lead quality;
- close rate;
- creative fatigue;
- capacity;
- cash constraints;
- inventory/service availability;
- experiment confidence.

Outputs are proposals such as:
- scale campaign;
- pause campaign;
- shift budget;
- produce new creative;
- test new hook;
- change offer;
- repair landing page;
- improve sales follow-up;
- launch retention campaign;
- do nothing until evidence improves.

It cannot autonomously exceed approved financial authority.

## Required judges

No producing agent self-certifies high-impact success.

- Creative Judge: quality, brand, policy, claim support.
- Website Judge: render, UX, responsive, accessibility, conversion.
- Tracking Judge: verifies IDs/events/UTMs/analytics before launch.
- Campaign Judge: verifies configuration and approval scope.
- Revenue Judge: reconciles measured revenue and economics.
- Experiment Judge: determines whether evidence supports the hypothesis.
- Compliance Judge: checks regulated/affiliate/advertising requirements where applicable.

## Safety and business truth rules

1. Never fabricate testimonials, customer results, reviews or endorsements.
2. Never claim revenue attribution without traceable evidence.
3. Never spend or materially increase budget outside approved authority.
4. Never publish unsupported medical, financial, legal or performance claims.
5. Never treat demo/seed data as measured business data.
6. Never optimize a metric while hiding a material negative downstream effect.
7. Never silently alter an offer, price, guarantee or contractual term.
8. Every high-impact action requires an audit trail.
9. Every learning promoted to durable memory must reference verified evidence.
10. Profit optimization remains constrained by brand, legal, customer and operational requirements.

## Maturity levels

### Level 0 — Disconnected tools
Content, website, CRM and analytics exist separately.

### Level 1 — Connected execution
Campaign IDs and customer records flow across systems.

### Level 2 — Measured loop
Revenue and downstream outcomes return to campaign/creative/page records.

### Level 3 — Experimenting system
Business OS creates controlled hypotheses and judges outcomes.

### Level 4 — Optimizing system
Business OS recommends resource allocation from verified economics.

### Level 5 — Governed adaptive growth system
TBrain coordinates CMO/CRO/CFO/COO/Customer Success, executes approved low-risk changes, escalates higher-risk decisions, measures outcomes and learns continuously.

## Golden Revenue Loop Test

A future integration cannot be called complete until Business OS can prove this scenario end-to-end:

1. Load a real business and approved business context.
2. Identify a measurable growth objective.
3. Research market/customer/competitor evidence.
4. Produce an evidence-backed offer/campaign hypothesis.
5. Build multiple creative concepts.
6. Produce UGC/content variants.
7. Build or update a tracked landing experience.
8. Pass creative, website, tracking and compliance judges.
9. Request required publishing/spend approval.
10. Publish through approved channels.
11. Capture leads into the canonical CRM identity.
12. Track qualification, opportunities and sales.
13. Reconcile revenue and contribution profit.
14. Attribute outcomes back to campaign/creative/offer/page.
15. Judge the hypothesis.
16. Record verified winning and losing patterns.
17. Recommend the next best action.
18. Execute an approved follow-up experiment.
19. Survive restart without losing mission state or duplicating side effects.
20. Produce an executive report showing evidence from strategy through profit.

## Architectural consequence

This Revenue Growth Loop becomes the canonical design for the Business OS growth system. Super Agents do not own isolated copies of marketing data. They coordinate through shared business entities, capability contracts, mission IDs, evidence, approvals and memory.

The system is not finished when it can create content.
The system is not finished when it can launch ads.
The system is not finished when it can build a website.

It is finished only when it can connect those activities to measurable business outcomes, learn from the result, and make the next decision better.
