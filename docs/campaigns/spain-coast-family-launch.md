# Campaign Draft: Spain’s Coast for the Whole Family

**Status:** Draft for review  
**Owner:** G-Brain → Marketing, Affiliate, Brand, UGC, Website, Social, and Analytics teams  
**Goal:** Build an honest, source-backed travel content funnel that earns through approved affiliate links and eligible platform monetization.

## Audience

Families planning a coastal Spain trip: parents seeking easy logistics, food experiences, comfortable stays, beach essentials, and activities suitable for mixed ages.

## Content package

1. **Cornerstone guide:** “A Family Guide to Spain’s Coast: Where to Eat, Stay, Swim, and Explore.”
2. **Destination sections:** Costa Brava, Costa del Sol, Valencia/Alicante, Mallorca, and the Basque coast.
3. **Product roundups:** family-friendly tours, beach gear, travel organization, sun protection, and clothing.
4. **Social series:** destination carousel, packing reel, family food checklist, hotel-selection tips, and “one day on the coast” short video.
5. **Email:** a practical Spain coast planning checklist with links to the full guide.

## Research requirements

- Verify current seasonal conditions, family access, transport, opening hours, and pricing before publication.
- Use live Viator or approved affiliate inventory and preserve the original product URL, image source, date checked, and commission/disclosure context.
- Use original, licensed, or user-provided images only; do not copy images from product pages without permitted usage.
- Mark affiliate relationships clearly and review Amazon/affiliate-program rules before including products.

## UGC brief

- 9:16: family beach arrival, food market moment, hotel-room organization, and beach-bag packing.
- 4:5: destination hero and product roundup cards.
- 16:9: website hero and long-form video thumbnail.
- Tone: warm, practical, trustworthy, accessible; avoid fabricated testimonials or guaranteed claims.

## Approval gates

- Research and product source check
- Brand voice and visual review
- Affiliate disclosure and platform compliance review
- Image/video rights and accessibility check
- Human approval before WordPress or social publishing

## Measurement

Track guide visits, outbound affiliate clicks, product conversions, email signups, saves/shares, video completion, and verified commission revenue. If a connector is unavailable, report the metric as pending rather than estimate it.

## First actions

- Run live Viator research for family coastal Spain experiences.
- Draft the cornerstone outline and five destination briefs.
- Create the UGC shot list and image-upload review queue.
- Prepare social variations and a calendar proposal.
- Return all work as drafts for operator approval.
