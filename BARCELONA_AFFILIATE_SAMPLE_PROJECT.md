# Barcelona Affiliate + Marketing Sample Project

Status: draft for review

## Goal

Turn the existing Royal Palace of Madrid guide into a higher-converting Barcelona travel hub that combines useful trip planning with clearly disclosed affiliate recommendations.

## Proposed flagship page

**Title:** Barcelona Travel Guide: Gaudí, Tapas, Beaches & the Perfect 3-Day Plan

**Hero promise:** A practical Barcelona itinerary with timed routes, ticket advice, neighborhood choices, and the travel gear that makes the trip easier.

### Page structure

1. Hero image, short promise, and “Build my 3-day route” CTA.
2. Quick facts: best trip length, airport transfer options, ideal neighborhoods, and reservation warnings.
3. Three-day itinerary:
   - Day 1: Sagrada Família, Sant Pau, and Eixample.
   - Day 2: Park Güell, Gràcia, and Casa Batlló.
   - Day 3: Gothic Quarter, El Born, Barceloneta, and sunset.
4. “Book before you fly” box linking to official attraction-ticket pages first.
5. Neighborhood comparison: Eixample, Gothic Quarter, Gràcia, El Born, and Barceloneta.
6. Product section: “Barcelona trip kit” with editorial reasons to consider each item.
7. Cost planner and packing checklist.
8. FAQ schema: tickets, pickpockets, transit, airport arrival, and beach timing.
9. Final CTA: download the itinerary / browse the Spain guide.

## Research-backed content anchors

- Barcelona Tourism identifies Sagrada Família, Park Güell, Casa Batlló, Santa Maria del Mar, and the Cathedral rooftops as key places of interest.
- Park Güell’s official site confirms its Gràcia-area location and current visitor information.
- Casa Batlló’s official visitor page confirms its Passeig de Gràcia location and official app.

## Affiliate product research queue

Use Amazon Associates links only after verifying current availability, price, and commission eligibility. Do not hard-code prices.

- Compact packing cubes — solves luggage organization for a 3-day city trip.
- Universal travel adapter with USB-C — useful for international visitors.
- Anti-theft crossbody or travel daypack — relevant to crowded sightseeing routes.
- Refillable insulated water bottle — useful for walking-heavy itineraries.
- Lightweight packable rain shell — practical shoulder-season item.
- Barcelona or Spain guidebook — supports pre-trip planning and earns a higher-intent click.

Each product card should include: product name, “why it fits Barcelona,” current price pulled at render time if supported, disclosure, and a labeled “Check on Amazon” link.

## Team assignments

- Website Builder Agent: reproduce the existing Madrid page’s visual language, improve hierarchy, add itinerary modules, and make the page mobile-first.
- Affiliate Strategist: validate product categories, disclosure placement, link tracking, and compliance.
- Marketing Strategist: define audience segments, lead magnet, email follow-up, and launch channels.
- SEO Specialist: map Barcelona keywords, internal links, title/meta copy, FAQ schema, and image alt text.
- Content Creator: write the itinerary and product explanations in the site’s established voice.
- Analytics Reporter: define events for CTA clicks, official-ticket clicks, Amazon clicks, and itinerary downloads.
- Reality Checker: review factual accuracy, broken links, affiliate disclosure, and mobile conversion flow.

## Acceptance test

- Page visually matches the existing Spain guide brand.
- Every attraction recommendation links to an official or clearly labeled source.
- Affiliate disclosure is visible before the first affiliate link.
- No invented product price, availability, rating, or commission claim.
- All primary CTAs work on mobile and desktop.
- Analytics events are observable without exposing personal data.

## Recommended first build

Create the Barcelona flagship page as a draft, link it from the Spain guide, and run a review before publishing. The first iteration should optimize for trust and itinerary usefulness; affiliate expansion can follow after click data is available.
